# Competition intelligence

Snapshot: October 6, 2026. Source identifiers resolve in the [source register](10-source-register.md). This is an operational reading of public requirements, not a determination of individual legal eligibility.

## Identity and mechanics

**AI4S Open Innovation: AI for Life Science** is an AI + organ-on-a-chip challenge within the fifth Pazhou Algorithm Competition. It accepts participant-defined problems and data, reviewed as project packages. The positioning categories are Model & Algorithm, Tool & Platform, and End-to-End System. All require the same deliverables. [K1]

The host confirms that real OoC data is encouraged, not compulsory. Public toxicity or imaging datasets are permissible with licenses and justified OoC relevance. Eligibility of proxy data does not establish target-domain scientific validity. [K3]

No fixed prediction-file metric or released hidden test set was verified. The advertised process is expert review and finalist defense. Generic leaderboard clauses in Kaggle's foundational rules need clarification for this format. [K1, K2]

## Material rule clauses

**Eligibility:** foundational rules require a Kaggle account and the older of 18 or local majority age, unless the sponsor agrees and obtains appropriate guardian consent. Residency/sanctions exclusions also apply. Check every member's situation; student status alone establishes neither eligibility nor ineligibility. Institutional work may require institutional consent. [K2]

**Ownership:** challenge-specific rules state joint ownership of submitted materials by team and committee, limit this to submitted material, preserve third-party rights, and require separate written agreement for specified commercial exploitation. Inventory background work and resolve this before submitting proprietary research. [K2]

**Private data:** lawful collaborative/private data is permitted, and unlawful disclosure is not required. The overview nevertheless requests reproduction without nonpublic datasets. Maintain a reproducible public core; label restricted validation separately pending clarification. [K1, K2]

**Code licenses:** foundational clauses discuss OSI-approved licenses without commercial-use restrictions for relevant shared/open-source code. Data, weights, and code require separate rights checks. Public access is not permission to redistribute. [K2]

**General registration:** Pazhou guidance requires matching dual-platform registration where applicable, recommends a teacher for student teams, and gives three days to respond to final/activity invitations. Domestic/overseas divisions are distinguished. Confirm whether this track's Kaggle registration plus form is sufficient. [P3]
